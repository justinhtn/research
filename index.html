    I watched a fine-tuned model get the right answer and invent the record it read it from

    A model fine-tuned on a simulated lab’s results, followed word by word. It often reaches the right answer while citing a lab record that doesn’t exist, and one word in its own reasoning can decide whether it gets there at all.
